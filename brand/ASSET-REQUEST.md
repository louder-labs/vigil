# Asset Request: Vigil App Icon and Mark

**Project:** Vigil, a free, non-denominational global prayer app (https://worldvigil.org)
**Owner:** Louder Labs, for stewards Brad McCorkle and McKayl
**Budget:** $0
**Needed:** one app mark plus the icon pack below

## The idea

Direction B, "Two Lights": two arcs of light from two places meet in one flame.
It stands for strangers keeping watch together. Tagline: "No one keeps vigil alone."

## Must

- Read instantly as **light, a candle flame, and connection** at 32 px and at 1024 px.
- Simple, symmetrical enough to feel calm, bold enough to work as a phone icon.
- Works on a dark background (primary) and holds up when placed on a light home screen.
- Flame looks like a real candle flame: rounded base, tapered tip.

## Must not

- No shape that can read as anatomical. In particular, no almond or teardrop shape nested inside a V or a curved notch. (A first draft failed on exactly this.)
- No crosses, crescents, stars, prayer beads, praying hands, buildings, or any one faith's symbol.
- No text inside the icon.
- No baked-in rounded corners or drop shadows outside the square. iOS and Android round the corners.
