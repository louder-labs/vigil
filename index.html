About Vigil

Vigil lets anyone who believes in God or a higher power light a prayer and see the world keeping watch together. It is free and always will be.

Kindness check. Every prayer is checked before it appears on the globe. Prayers with hate, cursing, threats, ads, links or personal details are not posted.

Privacy. We store your prayer, the first name you choose (if any), and the city you pick. We never store your exact location or your IP address. We count visits with Plausible, which uses no cookies and collects no personal data.

Get the app. Vigil installs from this link on any phone, tablet or computer. No app store, no account, no cost.
